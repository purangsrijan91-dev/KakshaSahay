# KakshaSahay: 120-Second Live Hackathon Presentation Script

> **Target Audience:** Hackathon Technical Judges & EdTech Evaluators  
> **Duration:** Exactly 120 Seconds (2 Minutes)  
> **Device Setup:** Low-end smartphone or laptop browser opened to `https://purangsrijan91-dev.github.io/KakshaSahay/`  
> **Precondition:** Disconnect Wi-Fi during the presentation to prove 100% offline operation.

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Frontline Reality: 1 Room, 1 Teacher, 3 Grades
* **Presenter Action:** Stand with device in hand. Point to the top trust bar and header.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Judges, over 38% of rural primary schools across India are single-teacher schools. Picture Masterji in a village in Uttar Pradesh: he has Grade 1, Grade 2, and Grade 3 sitting on the same jute mat in a single room. 
  > 
  > Most EdTech builds for 1-child-1-screen with broadband. But what does that lone teacher do when broadband is down and three grades need teaching at the exact same second?
  > 
  > We built **KakshaSahay (कक्षासहाय)** — a deterministic, offline-first multigrade classroom orchestration copilot."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:55] 3-Grade Live Orchestration & Explainable Rationale
* **Presenter Action:** Click the **`⚡ Demo Classroom Mode`** button on the orchestration bar. Then click **`🔍 Explain Allocation Rationale`**.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"With one tap, Masterji has a synchronized 15-minute multigrade plan. Look at the dashboard:
  > - **Grade 1** receives direct foundational phonics from the teacher.
  > - Simultaneously, **Grade 2** works independently on slate numeral decomposition.
  > - While **Grade 3** collaborates in front-row peer dyads solving market word problems.
  > 
  > When I click 'Explain Allocation Rationale', KakshaSahay shows the deterministic pedagogical reasoning why each group has its task — zero black-box mystery, zero hallucinations."*

---

### [0:55 – 1:20] Vernacular Scaffolding & Zero-Cost TLM
* **Presenter Action:** Scroll to **Card 2 (Bhasha Setu)**, select 'Math', click **`💡 Explain in Local Context`**. Then scroll to **Card 4 (Chalkboard TLM)** and enter `3` and `5` in the Number Train. Click **`Verify Train`**.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Second, dialect friction. Children speak Awadhi or Bundeli at home, but textbooks use standard Hindi. Bhasha Setu bridges textbook concepts with rural household analogies — completely offline.
  > 
  > Third, zero-cost physical materials: all practice exercises use what is already on the school floor — slates, blackboard chalk, dried neem twigs, and counting pebbles. The missing number train keeps Grade 2 engaged with zero screen addiction."*

---

### [1:20 – 1:45] Rapid Absentee Recovery (The Harvesting Problem)
* **Presenter Action:** Point to **Card 3 (Absenteeism Triage)** and the active roster.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"During wheat or paddy harvesting, 20% of rural kids miss school for three weeks. When they return, teachers have no time for 45-minute tests.
  > 
  > KakshaSahay provides a 2-minute oral catch-up screener. It instantly identifies the foundational gap and pairs the returning student with a front-row peer mentor for the morning session — no clinical labeling, just practical peer support."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:00] Engineering Rigor & Credibility
* **Presenter Action:** Open DevTools or show Network tab is OFFLINE. Show the footer with WCAG 2.2 AA.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Finally, engineering credibility:
  > - Every single core tool you saw is running **100% offline** via a hardware delta clock and client Web Audio synthesis.
  > - We have **16 automated Playwright E2E/WCAG tests**, **43 Jest unit tests**, and **60 pedagogical benchmark cases** passing in CI.
  > - And we make zero fake impact claims: our 4-week field pilot is documented and ready for state deployment.
  > 
  > KakshaSahay empowers the single teacher to teach three grades with confidence and dignity. Thank you."*
